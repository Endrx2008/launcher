Master Key System Usage Guide

Learn how to use the Skorpion_Os lite system, with function keys to control your system.

Master Key

Alt

ALT IS THE MASTER KEY

Open Launcher

Alt + F1

Press the Master Key and F1 together to open the launcher.

Open the fm

Alt + F2

Press the Master Key and F2 together to open pcmanfm.

Open Guide

Alt + F3

Press the Master Key and F3 together to open this guide.

Close Window

Alt + F5

Press the Master Key and F5 together to close programs.

Launch Firefox

Alt + F7

Press the Master Key and F7 together to launch Firefox browser.

Open Terminal

Alt + F10

Press the Master Key and F10 together to open the terminal.

Change configuration

Alt + F11

Press the Master Key and F11 together to change this configuration.

×

For closing this guide press (Alt + F5)

For opening this guide press (Alt + F3)
